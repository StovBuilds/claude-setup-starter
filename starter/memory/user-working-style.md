I prefer short answers with the result first and the detail after. Ask before any change that
touches more than a few files. UK spelling everywhere.

**Why:** I read replies on my phone between other things, and long answers get skimmed.

**How to apply:** lead with what happened or what you need from me, in one or two lines. Offer
more detail rather than including it by default.
